# AI Use Disclosure

AI tools are used extensively in the development of NFL RosterWire, including to assist with writing and revising code, tests, and documentation.

AI-generated suggestions are reviewed and tested as part of development, but may still contain mistakes. The maintainer is responsible for reviewing changes, validating the behavior of the project, and deciding what is included. This disclosure does not mean every individual change was written by AI or that AI output is independently verified to be error-free.
